Privacy Policy

Oasis Scout: Fenn's Adventure · Effective 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information Oasis Scout: Fenn's Adventure ("the game") handles, why, who receives it, and the choices parents and players have. The game is designed for children and families.

1. Who we are

The game is published on Google Play by the developer Ahmad-Odeh (legal name: Ahmad Waleed Ahmad Odeh), based in Amman, Jordan. In this policy, "we" means this developer.

For any privacy question or request, email ahmad.odeh1993@hotmail.com.

2. The short version

  • The game does not ask for a real name, email address, birthday, photo, contacts or location.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases in the current version.
  • Offline play works without sending anything to us or to anyone else.
  • For players under 16, online features stay off until a parent turns them on in Parent Settings.
  • There is no text chat. Online rooms use random animal names, such as "Falcon 42".
  • We do not sell personal information.

3. Information stored on the device

The game saves these on the player's device so that it can continue where the player left off:

  • A hero name chosen by the player. We suggest a nickname and ask players not to use a real full name.
  • An age group (under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or adult). The exact age and birthday are not stored.
  • Whether a parent allowed online play, and when.
  • Game progress, unlocked items and settings.
  • Random identifiers created by the game for its save file. They are not based on hardware identifiers.
  • Technical log files, which stay on the device unless you choose to share them with us.

4. Information used by online features

Online features are optional. They use Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, and only run for a player who is an adult or whose parent has allowed them. A player aged 16 or 17 may also use them without that step.

Epic's handling of information is described in the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

5. Microphone and voice chat

  • The microphone is used only for voice chat in a Play Together room with a code.
  • The device asks for microphone permission, and the player always joins a room muted.
  • The game does not record or save voice, and we do not receive voice recordings.
  • A parent can turn voice chat off at any time in Parent Settings, or deny the microphone permission in the device settings.
  • Please remind children to share room codes only with people they know, and never to say personal details aloud.

6. Children and parents

We collect only what is needed for the features a family chooses to use. Parents can, through Parent Settings (protected by a parent gate):

  • Allow or withdraw online play and cloud saves.
  • Turn voice chat on or off.
  • Change or remove the hero name.

A parent may also email us to ask about, correct or delete a child's information, or to withdraw consent. We may ask for enough information to find the right data, such as the player ID shown in the game. We will not ask for more than we need.

7. Keeping and deleting information

  • On the device: information stays until it is erased in the game or the game is uninstalled.
  • Cloud save: kept until it is deleted.
  • Voice and live gameplay: passed between players during the session and not saved by the game.
  • To delete the cloud save: open Parent Settings and choose "Delete my online data". Progress on the device stays. If you no longer have the game or the button is unavailable, email us the player ID shown in Parent Settings.
  • Online player ID: the button does not remove the anonymous player ID held by Epic Games. To ask about it, email us that ID.

8. Security

Cloud saves are private to the player's login, and we limit the game to the information described here. Same-Wi-Fi rooms and device transfers over Wi-Fi are not encrypted, so use them only on a home or other trusted network. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us if you believe there is a problem.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA, the UK and the United States, you or your child's parent may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to the use of, or receive a copy of personal information, and to withdraw consent. Email us to use these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on consent (including a parent's) for optional online features, and on our legitimate interest in making the game work for information stored on the device. Epic Games may process information in countries other than your own.

10. Changes to this policy

If we add features such as in-app purchases, or change how information is handled, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect. If a change requires new consent, we will ask for it.
